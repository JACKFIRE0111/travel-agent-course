## 3-Day Tokyo Plan

### Day 1 — Traditional Tokyo
- Morning: Meiji Shrine and a walk through Harajuku.
- Afternoon: Asakusa, Senso-ji, and the traditional shopping streets.
- Evening: Explore an izakaya area around Ueno or Yurakucho.

**Food:** Try sushi, tempura, and yakitori.

### Day 2 — Culture + Neighborhoods
- Morning: Tokyo National Museum.
- Afternoon: Ueno Park, then Yanaka for older streets and small shops.
- Evening: Dinner in Kagurazaka.

**Food:** Look for soba or a small kaiseki-style meal.

### Day 3 — Modern Tokyo
- Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market for breakfast.
- Afternoon: Shibuya and Omotesando, with time for a museum or gallery.
- Evening: Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building area or Shibuya for the final night.

**Food:** Try ramen for lunch and a higher-end Japanese dinner.

### Practical Tips
- Use an IC card for trains and local transit.
- Group each day's neighborhoods geographically to reduce travel time.
- Reserve popular restaurants and museums in advance.
- Avoid packing too many attractions into one day; Tokyo travel time adds up quickly.
